# Adding photographs and maps to The Price of Peace

## What to upload

Portraits for the twelve historical figures already included in the game:

**Israel:** Menachem Begin, Moshe Dayan, Ezer Weizman, Aharon Barak.

**Egypt:** Anwar Sadat, Mohamed Ibrahim Kamel, Boutros Boutros Ghali, Osama el Baz.

**United States:** Jimmy Carter, Cyrus Vance, Zbigniew Brzezinski, Alfred Atherton.

Additional images:

1. A labelled Sinai map showing Egypt, Israel and the Sinai Peninsula.
2. A map of the phased withdrawal from Sinai or the treaty security zones.
3. A photograph from the September 1978 Camp David summit.
4. A photograph of the 26 March 1979 treaty signing.
5. A photograph or scan of a key page of the accords or the peace treaty.

Optional images can be added to individual negotiation rounds in a later revision.

## How the game works now

The **Meet the teams** button opens the historical team cards. The **Maps and photographs** tab opens the gallery. Every missing photograph has a deliberate placeholder. No source is misrepresented. The game retains its existing illustrative Sinai diagram.

When images are provided, they can be cropped, optimised for mobile and placed in `assets/media/`. Their relative file paths are then assigned in `media.js`. Both the portrait and gallery interfaces will show them automatically. The game can still run without any photographs or internet connection.

## Recommended image specifications

* Portraits: portrait orientation, ideally at least 600 × 800 pixels.
* Maps and archival photographs: preferably at least 1200 pixels wide.
* Formats: JPEG, PNG or WebP.
* Keep the leaders' faces clear and avoid tight cropping of maps or historical documents.
* Where possible, include the archive, photographer, title and licence or permission details. Publicly viewable is not always the same as reusable.

## Historical image caption rules

Each photograph should identify what is shown and its date if known. A signing photograph from March 1979 must not be labelled as a September 1978 Camp David photograph. Maps should distinguish **1967 occupation**, **1979 treaty provisions** and **1982 withdrawal**, as applicable.

## Copyright and image permissions

User supplied images will be incorporated only once their use and any credit requirements are clear. Until then the placeholder design is intentional.
